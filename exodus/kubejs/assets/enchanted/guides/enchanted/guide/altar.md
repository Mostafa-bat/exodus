<Column alignItems="center" fullWidth={true}>
  ## <Color id="gold">Attuned Stone</Color>
</Column>

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="attuned_stone" />
  ### <Color id="aqua">Attuned Stone</Color>
</Row>

Before you can build the Altar, you need an Attuned Stone. This requires magical byproducts from the Witch's Oven and wood from magical trees — combining your progress in both witchcraft paths.

The Attuned Stone is also used in the Distillery and other advanced recipes.


<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<Column alignItems="center" fullWidth={true}>
  ## <Color id="gold">Building the Altar</Color>
</Column>

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="altar" />
  ### <Color id="aqua">Altar</Color>
</Row>

The Altar is built from <Color id="aqua">6 Altar blocks</Color> arranged in a specific pattern. The Altar generates power passively, which is increased by placing certain items and blocks on top of the altar.

Place the 6 Altar blocks in a 3x1x2 arrangement (3 long, 2 wide) to form the Altar structure.


<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

***

<Column alignItems="center" fullWidth={true}>
  ## <Color id="gold">Boosting Altar Power</Color>
</Column>

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

Nature blocks within 16 blocks buff power

Place these items on top of the Altar to increase its power generation:

<Row>
<ItemImage id="minecraft:skeleton_skull" scale="0.75" />
### <Color id="aqua">Skulls</Color>
</Row>

A skeleton skull gives +1x base power and recharge speed

A wither skeleton skull +2x base power and recharge speed

A player head gives +2.5x base power and 3x recharge speed

Place 1 of the above items on top of the Altar to boost its power.

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="candelabra" />
  ### <Color id="aqua">Light Sources</Color>
</Row>


A torch gives +0.5 recharge speed

A candle gives +1 recharge speed

A <ItemLink id="candelabra" /> gives +2 recharge speed


<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="chalice_filled" />
  ### <Color id="aqua">Chalice</Color>
</Row>

The Chalice can be filled with water and placed near the Altar to provide a significant power boost. Craft a <ItemLink id="chalice" /> and right-click water to fill it.

A standard chalice gives +1x base power

A filled chalice gives +2x base power, and is made by using <ItemLink id="redstone_soup" /> on a standard chalice
