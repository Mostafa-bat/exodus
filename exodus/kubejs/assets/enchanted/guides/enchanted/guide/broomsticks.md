<Row>
  <ItemImage id="broom" />
  ### <Color id="aqua">Broom</Color>
</Row>

The basic Broom is the first step. It is crafted from magical wood and serves as the base for the Enchanted Broomstick.


<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="flying_ointment" />
  ### <Color id="aqua">Flying Ointment</Color>
</Row>

Flying Ointment is a magical substance that must be applied to the Broom to grant it the power of flight. It is crafted through a Rite at the Altar, requiring Ritual Chalk circles and specific ingredients.

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="enchanted_broomstick" />
  ### <Color id="aqua">Enchanted Broomstick</Color>
</Row>

Combine the Broom with Flying Ointment to create the Enchanted Broomstick. Right-click to mount it and take flight!

The Enchanted Broomstick allows free flight in any direction. Look where you want to go and hold forward to fly. It is one of the most rewarding items to craft in the Enchanted mod.
