# <Color id="red">Augments</Color>

<Column alignItems="center" fullWidth={true}>
  # <Color id="red">Augments</Color>
  <ItemImage id="thermal:upgrade_augment_1" scale="2" />
  Augments are the upgrade system for every Thermal machine, Dynamo, Device and Cell. Install them in the augment slots to change speed, efficiency, output and energy handling.
</Column>

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<Color id="gold">Tip:</Color> Blocks are augmented in their own GUI. To augment a <Color id="aqua">portable item</Color> such as a Fluxbore or a Flux Capacitor, use a <ItemLink id="thermal:tinker_bench" />, which also charges and refills them.

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

***

<Column alignItems="center" fullWidth={true}>
  ## <Color id="gold">Integral Components (Tier Upgrades)</Color>
</Column>

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

Integral Components are the primary scaling mechanism. Each tier improves the block's base attributes, which in turn makes every other augment installed alongside it more effective. Fit these first.

Base Thermal provides three tiers:

| Tier | Augment |
|---|---|
| 1 | <ItemImage id="thermal:upgrade_augment_1" scale="0.5" /> <Color id="aqua">Hardened Integral Components</Color> |
| 2 | <ItemImage id="thermal:upgrade_augment_2" scale="0.5" /> <Color id="aqua">Reinforced Integral Components</Color> |
| 3 | <ItemImage id="thermal:upgrade_augment_3" scale="0.5" /> <Color id="aqua">Resonant Integral Components</Color> |

Resonant tier is where <ItemLink id="thermal:enderium_ingot" /> and <ItemLink id="thermal:lumium_ingot" /> come in.

<Color id="yellow">Note:</Color> <ItemLink id="thermal:enderium_ingot" /> unlocks once you reach <Color id="aqua">Muspelheim</Color>, so Resonant is a mid-game goal rather than an early one.

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

Thermal Extra continues the chain with two more:

| Tier | Augment |
|---|---|
| 4 | <ItemImage id="thermal_extra:upgrade_augment" scale="0.5" /> <Color id="aqua">Dragonsteel Integral Component</Color> |
| 5 | <ItemImage id="thermal_extra:abyssal_upgrade_augment" scale="0.5" /> <Color id="aqua">Abyssal Integral Component</Color> |

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

***

<Column alignItems="center" fullWidth={true}>
  ## <Color id="gold">Machine Augments</Color>
</Column>

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

Most of these are a trade rather than a straight gain. Read the second column before stacking them.

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

| Augment | Effect |
|---|---|
| <ItemImage id="thermal:machine_speed_augment" scale="0.5" /> <Color id="aqua">Flux Linkage Amplifier</Color> | Increases process speed, but <Color id="red">reduces RF efficiency</Color> |
| <ItemImage id="thermal:machine_efficiency_augment" scale="0.5" /> <Color id="aqua">Flux Efficiency</Color> | Increases RF efficiency, but <Color id="red">reduces process speed</Color> |
| <ItemImage id="thermal:machine_output_augment" scale="0.5" /> <Color id="aqua">Auxiliary Process Sieve</Color> | Increases non-primary (bonus) item output |
| <ItemImage id="thermal:machine_catalyst_augment" scale="0.5" /> <Color id="aqua">Catalytic Reclamation Chamber</Color> | Reduces catalyst consumption |
| <ItemImage id="thermal:machine_cycle_augment" scale="0.5" /> <Color id="aqua">Cyclical Processing</Color> | Feeds products back into the input when a process finishes |
| <ItemImage id="thermal:machine_null_augment" scale="0.5" /> <Color id="aqua">Auxiliary Cactus</Color> | Destroys excess non-primary outputs |

<Color id="gold">Tip:</Color> Speed and Efficiency pull directly against each other. Raise throughput with better Integral Components rather than trying to run both augment types at once.

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

***

<Column alignItems="center" fullWidth={true}>
  ## <Color id="gold">RF Coil Augments</Color>
</Column>

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

RF Coils improve energy handling on machines, Dynamos and Cells.

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

| Augment | Effect |
|---|---|
| <ItemImage id="thermal:rf_coil_augment" scale="0.5" /> <Color id="aqua">Expanded RF Coil</Color> | Increases energy capacity and transfer rate |
| <ItemImage id="thermal:rf_coil_storage_augment" scale="0.5" /> <Color id="aqua">Stabilized RF Coil</Color> | Greatly increases energy storage capacity |
| <ItemImage id="thermal:rf_coil_xfer_augment" scale="0.5" /> <Color id="aqua">High-Flux RF Coil</Color> | Greatly increases energy transfer rate |

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

***

<Column alignItems="center" fullWidth={true}>
  ## <Color id="gold">Dynamo Augments</Color>
</Column>

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

Augments for [Dynamos](dynamos.md).

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

| Augment | Effect |
|---|---|
| <ItemImage id="thermal:dynamo_output_augment" scale="0.5" /> <Color id="aqua">Auxiliary Reaction Chamber</Color> | Increases generation rate, but <Color id="red">reduces efficiency</Color> |
| <ItemImage id="thermal:dynamo_fuel_augment" scale="0.5" /> <Color id="aqua">Multi-cycle Injectors</Color> | Increases fuel efficiency |
| <ItemImage id="thermal:dynamo_throttle_augment" scale="0.5" /> <Color id="aqua">Excitation Field Limiter</Color> | Prevents RF being lost once the coil is saturated |

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

There are also three coil conversions that change what a Dynamo produces or how it delivers it: <Color id="aqua">Ender Frequency Tuner</Color>, <Color id="aqua">Beam Projection Array</Color> and <Color id="aqua">Steam Boiler Conversion</Color>.

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<Color id="gold">Tip:</Color> The Excitation Field Limiter is the one to fit first. Without it a Dynamo keeps consuming fuel when its buffer is already full.

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<Column alignItems="center" fullWidth={true}>
  ## <Color id="gold">Thermal Extra Tiers</Color>
</Column>

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

Thermal Extra re-issues the whole augment set in <Color id="aqua">five material tiers</Color>: Soul Infused, Twinite, Shellite, Dragonsteel and Abyssal. Each keeps the base augment's job and puts the material name in front, so a Dragonsteel Flux Linkage Amplifier does the same thing as the base one, harder.

Two families rename slightly: the Dynamo augments become <Color id="aqua">Multi Cycle Injectors</Color> and <Color id="aqua">Auxiliary Reaction Chamber</Color>.

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="thermal_extra:av_item_filter_augment" />
  ### <Color id="aqua">Advanced Item Filter Augment</Color>
</Row>

The one Thermal Extra augment with no base-game equivalent. It adds item filtering to a machine's input and output handling, useful anywhere a machine pulls from a shared inventory and you only want it to touch certain items.

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>
