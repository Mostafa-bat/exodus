  Botania provides a full suite of tools, weapons, armor, and trinkets, all powered by Mana. From basic Manasteel gear to endgame Terrasteel equipment and specialty weapons.

***

<Column alignItems="center" fullWidth={true}>
  ## <Color id="gold">Manasteel Equipment</Color>
</Column>

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

Manasteel is created by tossing Iron Ingots into a Mana Pool. Its tools and armour share most of their stats with Iron rather than Diamond, but come with noticeably better enchantability and durability, and every piece repairs itself from any Mana in your inventory, such as a <ItemLink id="mana_ring" /> or Mana Tablet.

Two of them have a right-click trick: the Pickaxe places a torch from your inventory, and the Axe plants a sapling.

<Column alignItems="center" fullWidth={true}>
  ## <Color id="gold">Terrasteel Equipment</Color>
</Column>

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

Created at the <ItemLink id="terra_plate" /> using enormous amounts of Mana. Terrasteel armour sits closer to Netherite than to Iron, and like Manasteel it heals its own damage from Mana in your inventory.

<ItemGrid>
  <ItemIcon id="terrasteel_helmet" />
  <ItemIcon id="terrasteel_chestplate" />
  <ItemIcon id="terrasteel_leggings" />
  <ItemIcon id="terrasteel_boots" />
</ItemGrid>

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="terra_sword" />
  ### <Color id="aqua">Terra Blade</Color>
</Row>

On par with a Netherite Sword for raw damage, and a swing can fire a beam that hits for as much as the melee strike would. It also draws on Mana for durability.

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="terra_pick" />
  ### <Color id="aqua">Terra Shatterer</Color>
</Row>

Its Rank comes from Mana, not from mining. Toss it onto a Mana Pool like a Mana Tablet and it soaks Mana up, climbing through ranks D, C, B, A, S and SS. Note that Mana it swallows can never be taken back out, so it is useless as storage.

Rank does not make it mine faster. It widens the Active Ability, toggled with a sneak-right-click, which breaks a large area at once: a narrow column at C, a square at B, and larger from there. Rank D has no ability at all. The tool takes durability per block broken, so bring a healthy Mana supply. A dropped Shatterer never despawns.

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<Column alignItems="center" fullWidth={true}>
  ## <Color id="gold">Alfsteel Equipment</Color>
</Column>

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

Alfsteel gear is the tier above Terrasteel. You do not craft it from scratch: take an existing piece of Terrasteel equipment to a Smithing Table with an <ItemLink id="mythicbotany:alfsteel_ingot" /> and the <ItemLink id="mythicbotany:alfsteel_template" />, exactly like a Netherite upgrade. A Terra Blade upgraded this way becomes the Alf Blade.

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="mythicbotany:mjoellnir" />
  ### <Color id="aqua">Mjöllnir</Color>
</Row>

Thor's hammer, built through a Rune Ritual rather than at a bench. It swings like any weapon but with a punishing attack cooldown, which the Hammer Mobility enchantment shortens. Right-click to throw it: a hit calls down a lightning strike and deals heavy damage, and Loyalty brings it back faster.

The hammer is far too heavy for a mortal. You need the <Color id="green">Absorption</Color> effect to hold it, since the gods drew their strength from Idun's golden apples, or an equipped <Color id="green">Ring of Thor</Color>. Let Absorption lapse and the hammer drops into the world where you stand. Sneak-right-click sets it down deliberately, and a right-click on a placed hammer picks it back up.

It takes most sword enchantments, which affect melee only, and most bow enchantments, which affect the throw.

<Column alignItems="center" fullWidth={true}>
  ## <Color id="gold">Special Weapons</Color>
</Column>

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="star_sword" />
  ### <Color id="aqua">Starcaller</Color>
</Row>

Swinging it drops a falling star on whatever point you are looking at, damaging anything standing there. Built from Ender Air and a Terra Blade, and any enchantments on that blade are lost in the process.

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="thunder_sword" />
  ### <Color id="aqua">Thundercaller</Color>
</Row>

The other Terra Blade upgrade. It gives up ranged attacks in exchange for chain lightning: striking one target in a crowd arcs to the hostile mobs around it.

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="glass_pickaxe" />
  ### <Color id="aqua">Vitreous Pickaxe</Color>
</Row>

Mines Glass blocks and gives them back as items. Cheap Silk Touch, for glass and nothing else.

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<Column alignItems="center" fullWidth={true}>
  ## <Color id="gold">Trinkets</Color>
</Column>

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="mana_ring" />
  ### <Color id="aqua">Band of Mana</Color>
</Row>

A wearable Mana Tablet. It behaves exactly like the tablet it is built from, and anything in your inventory that needs Mana draws from it. Plating it with a Terrasteel Ingot makes the Greater Band of Mana, which holds around four times as much.

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="aura_ring" />
  ### <Color id="aqua">Band of Aura</Color>
</Row>

Instead of storing Mana it slowly makes it, feeding the trickle into any Mana-holding items you are carrying. Plate it with Terrasteel for the Greater Band of Aura and a much faster rate. Pair it with a Band of Mana to keep your gear topped up.

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="travel_belt" />
  ### <Color id="aqua">Sojourner's Sash</Color>
</Row>

Spends a trickle of Mana to raise your movement speed, jump height and fall-damage resistance, and lets you step up one-block ledges like stairs as long as you are not sneaking.

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="super_travel_belt" />
  ### <Color id="aqua">Globetrotter's Sash</Color>
</Row>

The Sojourner's Sash rebuilt with Gaia Spirits and Elven resources. Same package, far higher speed, and the Sojourner's is not remotely competitive once you have one.

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="knockback_belt" />
  ### <Color id="aqua">Tectonic Girdle</Color>
</Row>

Cancels knockback from incoming attacks entirely, and all recoil from the Mana Blaster.

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="speed_up_belt" />
  ### <Color id="aqua">Planestrider's Sash</Color>
</Row>

Gives no speed bonus at all when you start moving. The longer you keep running the faster you get, climbing to ridiculous speeds, and the moment you stop the bonus resets to nothing.

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="magnet_ring" />
  ### <Color id="aqua">Ring of Magnetization</Color>
</Row>

Pulls nearby items towards you. Sneaking switches it off, as does standing in range of a <ItemLink id="solegnolia" />, and an item you have just thrown is ignored for a few seconds so you can still drop things on purpose.

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="bauble_box" />
  ### <Color id="aqua">Trinket Case</Color>
</Row>

A container for trinkets, with an interface for swapping what you have equipped. It also holds Rods and Mana-carrying items, though anything inside cannot supply Mana while it is in there.

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<Column alignItems="center" fullWidth={true}>
  ## <Color id="gold">Mythic Botany Rings</Color>
</Column>

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="mythicbotany:mana_ring_greatest" />
  ### <Color id="aqua">Greatest Band of Mana</Color>
</Row>

The top tier of Mana storage, well beyond the Greater Band of Mana.

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="mythicbotany:aura_ring_greatest" />
  ### <Color id="aqua">Greatest Band of Aura</Color>
</Row>

The top tier of passive Mana generation, well beyond the Greater Band of Aura.

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="mythicbotany:andwari_ring" />
  ### <Color id="aqua">Ring of Andwari</Color>
</Row>

The dwarf Andwari cursed his own treasure-multiplying ring so that Loki could never use it. You can lift that curse for a while: place a Gold Block and the ring pays out a reward, at a cost in Mana. The curse creeps back over time, so keep clearing it. Let it return in full and the ring will devour huge amounts of Mana and poison you. Find the ring in an Andwari Cave, which only generates in the golden fields biome of Alfheim.

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="mythicbotany:fire_ring" />
  ### <Color id="aqua">Ring of Muspelheim</Color>
</Row>

Makes you immune to every kind of fire damage and sets light to whatever you hit.

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="mythicbotany:ice_ring" />
  ### <Color id="aqua">Ring of Niflheim</Color>
</Row>

Hits land a brief but very strong Slowness on your target, and you become immune to cramming and to suffocation inside blocks.

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>
