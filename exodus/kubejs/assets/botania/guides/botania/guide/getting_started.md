# <Color id="green">Getting Started</Color>

<Column alignItems="center" fullWidth={true}>
  # <Color id="green">Getting Started</Color>
  <ItemImage id="botania:grass_seeds" scale="2" />

  Everything in Botania begins with <Color id="aqua">Mystical Flowers</Color>. Gather <Color id="green">Mystical Petals</Color> from them, craft your first flora at the <Color id="yellow">Petal Apothecary</Color>, and convert wood logs and stone into living materials with a Pure Daisy.
</Column>

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

***

<Column alignItems="center" fullWidth={true}>
  ## <Color id="gold">Mystical Flowers</Color>
  <ItemImage id="botania:white_mystical_flower" scale="2.0" />
</Column>

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<Color id="aqua">Mystical Flowers</Color> generate naturally throughout the world in 16 colors. They glow faintly and sparkle with particle effects. Collect them and place them in your crafting grid to obtain <Color id="green">Mystical Petals</Color>, this is the core crafting material for all of Botania's flora. <Color id="green">Mystical Petals</Color> can also be converted into vanilla dyes.

To grow more flowers, craft a <Color id="green"><ItemLink id="fertilizer" /></Color> by combining any 4x dyes with a bone meal. Use it on grass like regular bone meal to sprout a patch of Random Mystical Flowers.

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

***

<Column alignItems="center" fullWidth={true}>
  ## <Color id="gold">Petal Apothecary</Color>
  <ItemImage id="botania:apothecary_mossy" scale="2.0" /> 
</Column>

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<Column>
  The <Color id="yellow">Petal Apothecary</Color> is the crafting station for all <Color id="aqua">Generating, Miscellaneous and Functional Flora</Color>. Fill it with <Color id="blue">water</Color> by right-clicking with a bucket or tossing one above it. Then throw in the required <Color id="green">Mystical Petals</Color> and finalize the craft by tossing in any <Color id="dark_green">Seeds</Color>. Right-click with an empty hand to remove the last item added.
</Column>

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

***

<Column alignItems="center" fullWidth={true}>
  ## <Color id="gold">Pure Daisy</Color>
  <ItemImage id="pure_daisy" scale="2.0" />
</Column>

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<Column>
  A <Color id="yellow"><ItemLink id="pure_daisy"/></Color> is key for producing the materials you'll need as you progress through Botania. Place it in the world and it will <Color id="aqua">slowly purify the 8 blocks around it</Color>. <Color id="gold">Wood logs</Color> become <Color id="yellow"><ItemLink id="livingwood"/></Color> and <Color id="blue">Stone</Color> becomes <Color id="aqua"><ItemLink id="livingrock"/></Color>.
</Column>

Each conversion takes about a minute. The <Color id="yellow"><ItemLink id="pure_daisy"/></Color> also purifies several other materials: 
 - Netherrack -> Cobblestone
 - Soul Sand -> Sand
 - End Stone -> Cobbled Deepslate

And there's more! So make sure to check <Color id="dark_green">JEI</Color> for Additional Recipes.

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

***

<Column alignItems="center" fullWidth={true}>
  ## <Color id="gold">Wand of the Forest</Color>
  <ItemImage id="twig_wand" scale="2.0"/>
</Column>

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<Column>
  The <Color id="green"><ItemLink id="botania:twig_wand"/></Color> is the "wrench" of the mod, it has two modes. Switch between them by sneak-right-clicking the air.
</Column>


- **<Color id="aqua">Bind Mode</Color>:** Sneak-right-click a block to select it, then sneak-right-click a target to bind them together. This is how you connect a <Color id="dark_green"><ItemLink id="botania:mana_spreader"/></Color> to a <Color id="aqua"><ItemLink id="botania:mana_pool"/></Color>.
- **<Color id="blue">Function Mode</Color>:** Sneak-right-click to rotate blocks around the face you clicked. Most orientable blocks, chests included, can be turned this way.

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

***

<Column alignItems="center" fullWidth={true}>
  ## <Color id="gold">Runic Altar</Color>
  <ItemImage id="runic_altar" scale="2.0" />
</Column>

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<Column>
  The <Color id="green"><ItemLink id="botania:runic_altar"/></Color> handles the creation of <Color id="light_purple">Runes</Color>, vital components for <Color id="dark_purple">complex devices, advanced flora and late game content</Color> of Botania. Place ingredients onto the <Color id="green">altar</Color> by either right-clicking or dropping them above it, then point a <Color id="gold">Mana Spreader</Color> at it to supply <Color id="aqua">Mana</Color>.
</Column>


When the altar has absorbed enough <Color id="aqua">Mana</Color>, drop/right-click a piece of <Color id="dark_aqua"><ItemLink id="livingrock"/></Color> onto it and right-click it with the <Color id="dark_green"><ItemLink id="twig_wand"/></Color> to complete the craft.
