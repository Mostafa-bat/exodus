# <Color id="green">Functional Flora</Color>

<Column alignItems="center" fullWidth={true}>
  # <Color id="green">Functional Flora</Color>
  <ItemImage id="hopperhock" scale="2" />
  Functional Flora use Mana to perform various tasks. They must be near a Mana Pool to draw power.
</Column>

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

***

<Column alignItems="center" fullWidth={true}>
  ## <Color id="gold">Automation Flowers</Color>
</Column>

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="hopperhock" />
  ### <Color id="aqua">Hopperhock</Color>
</Row>

Picks up items around it and pushes them into adjacent inventories. Mana is optional and only extends its range. Hang an Item Frame on an inventory to restrict what may go in, and unlabelled inventories take whatever is left over. Sneak-right-click it with the <ItemLink id="twig_wand" /> in Function Mode to change its sorting rule.

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="rannuncarpus" />
  ### <Color id="aqua">Rannuncarpus</Color>
</Row>

Picks up placeable items lying nearby and sets them down as blocks across a wide radius. It only places onto one block type at a time, and you choose that type by putting it directly beneath the soil the flower is planted in. Item Frames on that same block filter what it will pick up. Mana is optional and only extends its range.

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="clayconia" />
  ### <Color id="aqua">Clayconia</Color>
</Row>

Moistens nearby Sand into Clay. Not free, but not expensive either.

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="orechid" />
  ### <Color id="aqua">Orechid</Color>
</Row>

Turns nearby Stone into ore. Which ore is random, with the rarer ones showing up less often. Very Mana-hungry, but it makes ore renewable.

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="marimorphosis" />
  ### <Color id="aqua">Marimorphosis</Color>
</Row>

Transmutes nearby Stone into one of the eight Metamorphic Stones. Every type can appear anywhere, but the biome weights which ones turn up most: Solite in deserts and savannas, Fuchsite in forests, Mycelite in mushroom fields and underground biomes, Rosy Talc in badlands, Gneiss in mountains, Talc in plains and beaches, Cataclasite in swamps and jungles, and Lunite in cold biomes.

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

***

<Column alignItems="center" fullWidth={true}>
  ## <Color id="gold">Growth and Breeding</Color>
</Column>

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="agricarnation" />
  ### <Color id="aqua">Agricarnation</Color>
</Row>

Turns Mana into fertilizer, speeding up the growth of plant life around it.

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="pollidisiac" />
  ### <Color id="aqua">Pollidisiac</Color>
</Row>

Feeds food items lying on the ground to animals in range, putting them in the mood to breed. Sneak-right-click it with the <ItemLink id="twig_wand" /> in Function Mode to switch between feeding adults, babies, or everything.

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

***

<Column alignItems="center" fullWidth={true}>
  ## <Color id="gold">Combat Flowers</Color>
</Column>

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="bellethorn" />
  ### <Color id="aqua">Bellethorne</Color>
</Row>

Slowly harms every living thing around it except players. Indiscriminate, so keep your livestock clear of it.

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="dreadthorn" />
  ### <Color id="aqua">Dreadthorne</Color>
</Row>

The pickier sibling of the Bellethorne: it harms adult animals only, which is what you want over a breeder that needs culling.

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="hyacidus" />
  ### <Color id="aqua">Hyacidus</Color>
</Row>

Poisons nearby mobs. Poison alone will not finish them, so it softens rather than kills.

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="tigerseye" />
  ### <Color id="aqua">Tigerseye</Color>
</Row>

Radiates a feline aura that stops nearby Creepers from exploding and sends them fleeing from players.

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<Column alignItems="center" fullWidth={true}>
  ## <Color id="gold">Entity Control</Color>
</Column>

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="tangleberrie" />
  ### <Color id="aqua">Tangleberrie</Color>
</Row>

Holds animals and monsters inside a circular ward, stopping them getting out. A cheap fence for a mob farm.

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="jiyuulia" />
  ### <Color id="aqua">Jiyuulia</Color>
</Row>

The inverse of the Tangleberrie: it keeps animals and monsters out of a circular area rather than penning them in.

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="medumone" />
  ### <Color id="aqua">Medumone</Color>
</Row>

Projects a slowing field that stops nearby non-player entities dead in their tracks.

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<Column alignItems="center" fullWidth={true}>
  ## <Color id="gold">Utility Flowers</Color>
</Column>

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="fallen_kanade" />
  ### <Color id="aqua">Fallen Kanade</Color>
</Row>

Grants Regeneration to players and tamed pets nearby.

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="bergamute" />
  ### <Color id="aqua">Bergamute</Color>
</Row>

Halves the volume of sounds in a small radius, and the effect stacks with other Bergamutes. Horns and Drums will not break blocks inside its range, and it muffles vibrations passing through.

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="bubbell" />
  ### <Color id="aqua">Bubbell</Color>
</Row>

Holds back the sea with a dome of air roughly 12 blocks across. It needs a steady Mana supply to stay up, so charge it before you need it.

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="loonium" />
  ### <Color id="aqua">Loonium</Color>
</Row>

Summons dungeon loot for a hefty pile of Mana, but every item arrives clutched by an unusually strong monster that you have to kill for it.

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="solegnolia" />
  ### <Color id="aqua">Solegnolia</Color>
</Row>

Jams the Ring of Magnetization. Items in its range will not fly to a ring wearer, and a wearer standing in range cannot pull anything at all. Costs no Mana, and it is the standard fix for a magnet ring wrecking a dropped-item automation setup.

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<Column alignItems="center" fullWidth={true}>
  ## <Color id="gold">Mythic Botany Flora</Color>
</Column>

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

Mythic Botany adds several functional flowers. All have floating variants.

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="mythicbotany:aquapanthus" />
  ### <Color id="aqua">Aquapanthus</Color>
</Row>

Spends a trickle of Mana to keep nearby Cauldrons and Petal Apothecaries topped up with water, along with a few other fluid containers. It does not place water in the world.

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="mythicbotany:exoblaze" />
  ### <Color id="aqua">Exoblaze</Color>
</Row>

Keeps the fuel slot of nearby Brewing Stands filled using Mana, so you never need to hand it Blaze Powder again.

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="mythicbotany:hellebore" />
  ### <Color id="aqua">Hellebore</Color>
</Row>

Spends Mana to stop Piglins and Hoglins in range from zombifying outside the Nether, which makes an overworld piglin farm practical.

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="mythicbotany:petrunia" />
  ### <Color id="aqua">Petrunia</Color>
</Row>

Triggers rune rituals. Plant it near the Central Rune Holder and keep it supplied with Mana, and it will set the ritual off.

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="mythicbotany:feysythia" />
  ### <Color id="aqua">Feysythia</Color>
</Row>

Feeds on Fey Dust and Fey Gems from the Feywild mod. That mod is not part of this pack, so the Feysythia has nothing to eat here and will sit idle.

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>
