# <Color id="green">Mana</Color>

<Column alignItems="center" fullWidth={true}>
  # <Color id="green">Mana</Color>
  <ItemImage id="mana_spreader" scale="2" />

  <Color id="aqua">Mana</Color> is the lifeblood of Botania. Generated by flowers, transported by Spreaders or Sparks, stored inside Pools, it is consumed by devices and/or functional flora. Understanding how to manipulate <Color id="aqua">Mana</Color> is key to mastering this mod.
</Column>

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

***

<Column alignItems="center" fullWidth={true}>
  ## <Color id="gold">Mana Spreader</Color>
  <ItemImage id="mana_spreader" scale="2.0" />
</Column>

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<Column>
  The <Color id="green"><ItemLink id="mana_spreader" /></Color> is the primary <Color id="aqua">Mana</Color> transport block. It points to a target and fires <Color id="aqua">Mana</Color> bursts at them. Use the <ItemLink id="twig_wand" /> to link a Spreader to a <ItemLink id="mana_pool" /> or any specific block.
</Column>

A spreader only fires while its target can accept <Color id="aqua">Mana</Color>, and it will not fire again until the previous burst lands. A redstone signal pauses it.

Variants exist. The plain <ItemLink id="mana_spreader" /> is made from <ItemLink id="livingwood" />. The <ItemLink id="redstone_spreader" /> fires a <Color id="aqua">Mana</Color> burst on a redstone pulse regardless of a valid target. The <ItemLink id="elven_spreader" /> uses Dreamwood and carries more <Color id="aqua">Mana</Color>, faster, and further before <Color id="aqua">Mana</Color> Loss sets in, though it cannot be made into a Redstone Pulse variant. The <ItemLink id="gaia_spreader" /> is the late game variant of a spreader, having better transfer stats than the previously mentioned <ItemLink id="elven_spreader" />.

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<Column alignItems="center" fullWidth={true}>
  ## <Color id="gold">Mana Pool</Color>
  <ItemImage id="mana_pool" scale="2.0" />
</Column>

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<Column>
  The <Color id="green"><ItemLink id="mana_pool" /></Color> stores <Color id="aqua">Mana</Color> received from Spreaders or Sparks and provides it automatically to functional flowers within range. Items thrown into a filled <ItemLink id="mana_pool" /> can be transformed via <Color id="aqua">Mana</Color> Infusion recipes, check <Color id="dark_green">JEI</Color> for the full recipe list.
</Column>

Just like Spreaders, there are Mana Pool Variants:
  - Diluted
  - Fabulous
  - Creative

Each one capable of storing more mana than the other in a crescent order, with the Default one sitting right between the Diluted and the Fabulous one.

The <ItemLink id="diluted_pool" /> holds a hundredth of what a normal pool does, which makes it a useful small buffer and far easier to read at a glance.

Use a <ItemLink id="mana_tablet" /> to carry <Color id="aqua">Mana</Color> around: toss it into a pool to fill/drain them, and sneak-right-click the pool with the <ItemLink id="twig_wand" /> to flip which way the <Color id="aqua">Mana</Color> flows.

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<Column alignItems="center" fullWidth={true}>
  ## <Color id="gold">Mana Lenses</Color>
  <ItemImage id="lens_normal" scale="2.0" />
</Column>

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<Column>
  You can attach <Color id="aqua">Mana</Color> Lenses to <Color id="aqua">Mana</Color> Spreaders to modify how their <Color id="aqua">Mana</Color> bursts behave. The plain <ItemLink id="lens_normal" /> does nothing on its own. Two lenses can be merged into one with a Slimeball or Honey Bottle in the crafting grid, though not every pairing works and you cannot merge two of the same type.
</Column>

Common lens types include:
- **<Color id="aqua">Velocity</Color>:** much faster bursts, but they carry less and lose <Color id="aqua">Mana</Color> sooner.
- **<Color id="red">Potency</Color>:** double the <Color id="aqua">Mana</Color> per burst, but slower and with faster loss once it starts.
- **<Color id="green">Efficiency</Color>:** slightly delays <Color id="aqua">Mana</Color> Loss and greatly slows the rate of it.
- **<Color id="gray">Bore</Color>:** the burst spends its own <Color id="aqua">Mana</Color> to break blocks it hits.
- **<Color id="light_purple">Phantom</Color>:** the burst passes through blocks, at the cost of losing <Color id="aqua">Mana</Color> sooner.
- **<Color id="dark_purple">Warp</Color>:** a burst hitting a Force Relay teleports to that relay's target, keeping its momentum but losing its <Color id="aqua">Mana</Color> for good.

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<Column alignItems="center" fullWidth={true}>
  ## <Color id="gold">Sparks</Color>
  <ItemImage id="spark" scale="2.0" />
</Column>

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<Column>
  <Color id="green">Sparks</Color> are by far the fastest method to transport <Color id="aqua">Mana</Color>, but to do so you'll need to combine them with Spark Augments. place one on a <ItemLink id="mana_pool" /> and another on another Mana Pool or any nearby block that accepts them, place a Recessive Augment on the First one for the second to be able to draw on the first's reserves. Sneak-right-click a <ItemLink id="spark" /> with the <ItemLink id="twig_wand" /> to remove it, or plain right-click to see which Sparks it can reach and if it has augments installed. Dyed Sparks only ever talk to Sparks of the same colour.
</Column>

Spark Augments change how a <ItemLink id="spark"/> treats its neighbours:

- **<Color id="red">Dominant</Color>:** pulls <Color id="aqua">Mana</Color> out of nearby unaugmented Spark pools until its own is full
- **<Color id="blue">Recessive</Color>:** empties its own pool into nearby unaugmented or Dispersive Spark pools
- **<Color id="gold">Dispersive</Color>:** drains its pool to charge <Color id="aqua">Mana-holding</Color> items carried by nearby players
- **<Color id="gray">Isolated</Color>:** ignores Dominant and Recessive Sparks, but still feeds blocks on the Spark network

<Column alignItems="center" fullWidth={true}>
  ## <Color id="gold">Other Mana Devices</Color>
</Column>

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="mana_tablet" />
  ### <Color id="aqua">Mana Tablet</Color>
</Row>

Portable <Color id="aqua">Mana</Color> storage. Drop into a <ItemLink id="mana_pool" /> to charge or drain it. Supplies <Color id="aqua">Mana</Color> to items in your inventory that need it.

Sneak-right-click the pool with the <ItemLink id="twig_wand" /> to flip which way the <Color id="aqua">Mana</Color> flows.

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="mana_mirror" />
  ### <Color id="aqua">Mana Mirror</Color>
</Row>

Sneak-right-click a <ItemLink id="mana_pool" /> with the mirror to bind it. Items in your inventory that need <Color id="aqua">Mana</Color> then draw straight from that pool, wherever you are. Binding is the only way it moves <Color id="aqua">Mana</Color>, so it cannot be used to fill or empty pools by hand.

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="mana_void" />
  ### <Color id="aqua">Mana Void</Color>
</Row>

Place under a <ItemLink id="mana_pool" /> to destroy excess <Color id="aqua">Mana</Color>.

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="mana_distributor" />
  ### <Color id="aqua">Mana Splitter</Color>
</Row>

Splits the <Color id="aqua">Mana</Color> it receives from bursts evenly into <Color id="aqua">Mana</Color> Pools placed on its adjacent sides.

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="prism" />
  ### <Color id="aqua">Mana Prism</Color>
</Row>

Right-click it with any <Color id="aqua">Mana</Color> Lens to slot the lens in. Bursts flying through the prism take on that lens's colour and effect, letting you switch a burst's behaviour mid-flight. The prism has no physical presence, so entities and bursts pass straight through, and a redstone signal disables it.

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="mana_detector" />
  ### <Color id="aqua">Mana Detector</Color>
</Row>

Emits a redstone signal when a <Color id="aqua">Mana</Color> burst passes through it.
