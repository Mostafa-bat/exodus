<Column alignItems="center" fullWidth={true}>
  ## <Color id="gold">Runewood</Color>
</Column>

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="runewood_log" />
  <ItemImage id="exposed_runewood_log"/> 
  ### <Color id="aqua">Runewood</Color>
</Row>

Runewood Trees are found naturally in the world. Their wood is the basic building material for most magical devices and structures.

You will also see <Color id="dark_purple">Exposed Runewood logs</Color> in the trees once the sapling grows. You can harvest sap from these logs using glass bottles.

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<Column alignItems="center" fullWidth={true}>
  ## <Color id="gold">Soulstone</Color>
</Column>

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="raw_soulstone" />
  ### <Color id="aqua">Soulstone</Color>
</Row>

Soulstone doesn't naturally generate in Helheim. You will need to seek out Enderslime Crystals <ItemLink id="tconstruct:ender_slime_crystal"/> and throw it into the void to get the raw soulstone you seek.

Once you have the raw soulstone, you can smelt it into <ItemLink id="processed_soulstone" />. Soulstone is used in many crafting recipes and is a core material alongside Runewood.

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>
