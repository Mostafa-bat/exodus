***

<Column alignItems="center" fullWidth={true}>
  ## <Color id="gold">Performing Rituals</Color>
</Column>

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="ritual_brazier" />
  ### <Color id="aqua">Ritual Brazier</Color>
</Row>

Rituals require a <Color id="green">Ritual Brazier</Color> and a <Color id="green">Ritual Tablet</Color>. Place the brazier, then use a tablet on it to set the ritual. Before activating, you can use augment items on the brazier one at a time to modify the ritual's behavior. Once augments are set, right click with an empty hand to activate. Most rituals require <Color id="green">Source</Color> from a nearby jar.

<ItemLink id="brazier_relay" /> blocks can bind a ritual's effect to a new location within 15 blocks of the brazier. Applying a redstone signal to a running brazier disables the ritual.

<Column alignItems="center" fullWidth={true}>
  ## <Color id="gold">Notable Rituals</Color>
</Column>

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

- **Ritual of Awakening** - Creates Weald Walkers, Amethyst Golems, and Bookwyrms from appropriate creatures or blocks
- **Ritual of Binding** - Obtains Bound Scripts for summoning familiars
- **Ritual of Containment** - Captures mobs into Mob Jars
- **Ritual of Flight** - Grants creative flight in an area around the brazier
- **Ritual of Harvest** - Auto-harvests mature crops nearby, depositing into an adjacent inventory
- **Ritual of Fertility** - Breeds nearby animals automatically, idle once twenty animals are present
- **Ritual of Flowering** - Scatters flowers and grass through the area, or cactus and dead bushes if augmented with sand
- **Ritual of Overgrowth** - Periodically bonemeals the area, or speeds up baby animals if augmented with a Bone Block
- **Ritual of Sanctuary** - Denies hostile spawns in a 32 block radius, extendable to 128 with rotten flesh
- **Ritual of Disintegration** - Destroys nearby monsters for double experience, but no item drops
- **Ritual of Restoration** - Heals the living, harms undead, and instantly cures Zombie Villagers
- **Ritual of Sunrise / Moonfall** - Controls the time of day
- **Ritual of Cloudshaping** - Controls the weather
- **Ritual of Scrying** - Reveals nearby ores through blocks
- **Ritual of Warping** - Warps nearby entities to an inscribed Warp Scroll used as an augment
- **Ritual of Wilden Summon** - Summons a Wilden boss fight for rare drops

<Column alignItems="center" fullWidth={true}>
  ## <Color id="gold">Familiars</Color>
</Column>

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

Summon familiars via the <Color id="green">Ritual of Binding</Color> performed near the appropriate creature. The ritual produces a Bound Script, which you use to learn the familiar. Once learned, familiars follow you and provide passive bonuses.

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

    - **Starbuncle Familiar** - Speed 2, and a gold nugget grants Scrying for Gold Ore

    - **Whirlisprig Familiar** - Halves Earth glyph costs and adds saturation from food
  </Column>
  <Column>

    - **Wixie Familiar** - Extends your potions and throws harmful ones at enemies

  </Column>

Ars Elemental adds two more:

<ItemGrid>
  <ItemIcon id="ars_elemental:siren_familiar" />
  <ItemIcon id="ars_elemental:firenando_familiar" />

- **Siren Familiar** - +2 Water spell damage and Dolphin's Grace II while swimming. Bind near a Siren, which you first befriend with a sea pickle.
- **Flarecannon Familiar** - +2 Fire spell damage and 20% cheaper projectile spells. Feed it Magma Cream for Fire Resistance.

  ## <Color id="gold">Summon Focus</Color>
</Column>

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="summon_focus" />
  ### <Color id="aqua">Summon Focus</Color>
</Row>

A casting focus that enhances summoned creatures. Grants summons additional duration, strength, and speed. Spells that target you are also copied to nearby summons while the focus is equipped.
