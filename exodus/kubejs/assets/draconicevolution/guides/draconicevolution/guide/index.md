  # <Color id="red">Draconic Evolution</Color>

  <ItemImage id="dragon_heart" scale="2" />

  Draconic Evolution is an endgame technology mod built on Draconium. It has four tiers of modular tools and armour, massive Energy Cores, Fusion Crafting, a Draconic Reactor for enormous power output, teleportation via Dislocators, and the mod's own boss, the Chaos Guardian.

  The pack also ships <Color id="aqua">Draconic Additions</Color>, which adds Harnesses and Necklaces for extra module slots, Tick Accelerators, and the Chaos Heart line.
</Column>

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25" />

<Color id="light_purple">Pack change:</Color> the Chaos Guardian no longer disables your flight or traps you in its arena. See [Chaos Guardian](chaos_guardian.md) for the fight in full.

<Color id="light_purple">Pack change:</Color> Fusion Crafting sits on the critical path for the main storyline. <ItemLink id="ftb:aesirium" />, the item that summons Nidhoggr, can only be made in the Fusion Crafting Core. See [Fusion Crafting](fusion_crafting.md).

<Color id="red">Note:</Color> Draconium Ore only generates in <Color id="green">Muspelheim</Color> in this pack, End Comets are switched off, and all three chestpieces are locked until you finish the main storyline.
