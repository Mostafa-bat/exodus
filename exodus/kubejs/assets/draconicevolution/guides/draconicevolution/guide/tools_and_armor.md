# <Color id="red">Tools & Armor</Color>

<Column alignItems="center" fullWidth={true}>
  # <Color id="red">Tools & Armor</Color>
  <ItemImage id="draconic_sword" scale="2" />

  Draconic Evolution's gear is fully modular. Tools exist at <Color id="aqua">Wyvern</Color>, <Color id="light_purple">Draconic</Color> and <Color id="red">Chaotic</Color> tiers, and everything runs on stored energy rather than durability.
</Column>

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25" />

***

<Column alignItems="center" fullWidth={true}>
  ## <Color id="gold">Modular System</Color>
</Column>

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

Every DE tool and chestpiece has a module grid. Open the <Color id="aqua">Module Configuration GUI</Color> with the mod's keybind and slot modules into the grid. Higher tiers have larger grids, so tier is really about how many modules you can carry as much as raw stats.

Gear runs on stored <Color id="aqua">FE</Color>, so anything that charges a battery charges DE gear. The mod's screens write it as OP, which is only a label, not a different unit.

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25" />

***

<Column alignItems="center" fullWidth={true}>
  ## <Color id="gold">Armor</Color>
</Column>

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<Color id="yellow">Draconic Evolution only has chest armour.</Color> There is no DE helmet, leggings or boots on this version, so pair a chestpiece with whatever else you are wearing. The chestpiece is what carries your armour modules.

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25" />

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="wyvern_chestpiece" />
  ### <Color id="aqua">Wyvern Chestpiece</Color>
</Row>

The entry tier. Enough grid space for a shield, flight and a handful of utility modules.

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="draconic_chestpiece" />
  ### <Color id="light_purple">Draconic Chestpiece</Color>
</Row>

A large step up in grid size, which is what lets you run a serious shield alongside flight and utility modules at the same time.

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="chaotic_chestpiece" />
  ### <Color id="red">Chaotic Chestpiece</Color>
</Row>

The largest grid in the mod, and the only realistic home for a fully specced shield build.

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<Color id="red">Pack gate:</Color> all three chestpieces require the <Color id="aqua">draconic stage</Color>, granted by the <Color id="gold">Restored Asgard</Color> quest. You will have the tools long before you can wear the armour.

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25" />

<Column alignItems="center" fullWidth={true}>
  ## <Color id="gold">Modules</Color>
</Column>

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

Modules come in <Color id="aqua">four</Color> tiers, one more than the tools: <Color id="aqua">Draconium</Color>, <Color id="aqua">Wyvern</Color>, <Color id="light_purple">Draconic</Color> and <Color id="red">Chaotic</Color>. The Draconium tier is a cheap starter set covering AOE, Auto Feed, Damage, Energy, Jump and Speed, so you can begin modding gear before you have any Wyvern Cores.

Every module is built around a <ItemLink id="module_core" />.

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

| Module | Effect |
|---|---|
| <Color id="aqua">Energy Capacity</Color> | Raises the item's internal energy storage |
| <Color id="aqua">Damage</Color> / <Color id="aqua">AOE</Color> | More weapon damage, wider mining and attack area |
| <Color id="aqua">Speed</Color> | Faster mining and movement |
| <Color id="aqua">Shield Boost</Color> | More shield hit points |
| <Color id="aqua">Shield Controller</Color> | Tunes shield behaviour and recovery |
| <Color id="green">Flight</Color> | Creative-style flight. Available from <Color id="aqua">Wyvern</Color> tier upward |
| <Color id="green">Undying</Color> | Survives a killing blow at a large energy cost |
| <Color id="green">Energy Link</Color> | Draws power wirelessly from your Energy Core |
| <Color id="green">Tree Harvest</Color> | Fells a whole tree in one swing |
| <Color id="green">Auto Feed</Color> | Eats from your inventory to keep you fed |
| <Color id="green">Ender Collection</Color> | Sends drops straight into storage |
| <Color id="green">Night Vision</Color>, <Color id="green">Step Assist</Color>, <Color id="green">Jump Boost</Color>, <Color id="green">Aqua Adaptation</Color> | Quality of life |
| <Color id="green">Junk Filter</Color>, <Color id="green">Mining Stabilizer</Color> | Filter unwanted drops, remove mining slowdown |
| <Color id="green">Projectile Modifier</Color> family | Bow accuracy, damage, velocity, penetration and gravity compensation |

<Color id="gold">Tip:</Color> Flight is not locked to Draconic tier. A Wyvern Flight module in a Wyvern Chestpiece works, it just leaves less grid space for everything else.
