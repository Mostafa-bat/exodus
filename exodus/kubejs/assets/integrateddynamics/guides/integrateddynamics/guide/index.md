<Column alignItems="center" fullWidth={true}>
  # <Color id="aqua">Integrated Dynamics</Color>

  <ItemImage id="logic_programmer" scale="2" />

  Integrated Dynamics is the go-to mod for automating your logic systems. It provides a deep, immersive logic programming experience with support for almost all elements of Minecraft and its modifications. Build networks, define variables, and create complex automation logic with code-like card configurations.
</Column>

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.5"/>

***

<Column alignItems="center" fullWidth={true}>
  ## <Color id="gold">Progression Overview</Color>
</Column>

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

1. Find Menril Trees or craft its saplings and harvest Menril Logs.
2. Craft a <ItemLink id="squeezer" /> and <ItemLink id="drying_basin" /> to process Menril resources.
3. Build a network with <ItemLink id="cable" /><Color id="aqua">s</Color> to connect your machines and storage.
4. Craft <ItemLink id="variable" /><Color id="aqua">s</Color> and different types of readers to extract information from machines, inventories, and many other things.
5. Use a <ItemLink id="logic_programmer" /> to create processing chains using variables.
