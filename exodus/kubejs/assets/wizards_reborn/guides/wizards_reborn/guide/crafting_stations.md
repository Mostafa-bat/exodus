<Column alignItems="center" fullWidth={true}>
  ## <Color id="gold">Arcane Workbench</Color>
</Column>

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="arcane_workbench" />
  ### <Color id="aqua">Arcane Workbench</Color>
</Row>

The Arcane Workbench is the primary crafting station for Wizard's Reborn. It is a crafting table with <Color id="aqua">extra slots</Color> below the grid, and it spends Wissen on every craft. Connect it to your Wissen network to power it.

Most of the mod's blocks are made here, including the Light Emitter, Light-Transfer Lens and Runic Pedestal.


<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

***

<Column alignItems="center" fullWidth={true}>
  ## <Color id="gold">Wissen Crystallizer</Color>
</Column>

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="wissen_crystallizer" />
  ### <Color id="aqua">Wissen Crystallizer</Color>
</Row>

The Wissen Crystallizer crystallises Wissen into solid form. It is the only source of <Color id="aqua">Crystal Seeds</Color>, and it is also where you reassemble Fractured Crystals into whole ones and craft Arcane Linen Seeds. Everything to do with spellcasting starts here.


<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

***

<Column alignItems="center" fullWidth={true}>
  ## <Color id="gold">Arcane Wood Mortar</Color>
</Column>

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="arcane_wood_mortar" />
  ### <Color id="aqua">Arcane Wood Mortar</Color>
</Row>

The Mortar grinds plants into alchemy ingredients. It is a held item, not a block: <Color id="yellow">hold the Mortar in one hand and the ingredient in the other</Color>, then right-click to grind.

It turns any flower into <ItemLink id="petals" />, mushrooms into Ground Mushrooms, and wheat or Arcane Linen into flour. Petals are the ingredient the whole alchemy chain rests on, so keep a stock.

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

***

<Column alignItems="center" fullWidth={true}>
  ## <Color id="gold">Jeweler Table</Color>
</Column>

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="jeweler_table" />
  ### <Color id="aqua">Jeweler Table</Color>
</Row>

An arcacite grindstone that polishes crystals into <Color id="aqua">Faceted Crystals</Color>, which refract better and let a wand cast stronger spells. It also applies decorative trims to arcane gear.

This one comes later than the others, since it needs Arcacite from the alchemy chain.
