<Column alignItems="center" fullWidth={true}>
  # <Color id="dark_purple">Getting Started</Color>

  <ItemImage id="arcanum" scale="2" />

  Everything in Wizard's Reborn begins with Arcanum, a blue crystal that generates in underground caves. Mine it, grind it, and use it to transmute the world around you.

  Craft the <ItemLink id="arcanemicon" /> from a Book and any Arcanum before you go further. It is the mod's own reference book and covers everything in detail.
</Column>

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

***

<Column alignItems="center" fullWidth={true}>
  ## <Color id="gold">Arcanum Resources</Color>
</Column>

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="arcanum" />
  ### <Color id="aqua">Arcanum</Color>
</Row>

Arcanum is the foundational resource of Wizard's Reborn. Mine Arcanum Ore underground to obtain Arcanum, then grind it into <ItemLink id="arcanum_dust" />.

Almost every recipe in the mod takes one or the other, so it is worth setting up a renewable supply early. Planting an <ItemLink id="arcanum_seed" /> and transmuting it grows more Arcanum, which saves you a lot of caving later.

<Color id="yellow">Transmutation:</Color> hold Arcanum Dust and right-click the block you want to change. This is how you make Arcane Wood Saplings, Arcane Gold Ore, and Crystal Seeds start growing.

<Column alignItems="center" fullWidth={true}>
  ## <Color id="gold">Arcane Wood</Color>
</Column>

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="arcane_wood_sapling" />
  ### <Color id="aqua">Arcane Wood Sapling</Color>
</Row>

Use <ItemLink id="arcanum_dust" /> on any ordinary sapling to transmute it into an Arcane Wood Sapling. Plant and grow it to harvest Arcane Wood, which is used in many Wizard's Reborn crafting recipes.

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

***

<Column alignItems="center" fullWidth={true}>
  ## <Color id="gold">Arcane Gold</Color>
</Column>

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="raw_arcane_gold" />
  ### <Color id="aqua">Arcane Gold</Color>
</Row>

Use <ItemLink id="arcanum_dust" /> on a Gold Ore block in the world to transmute it into Arcane Gold Ore. Deepslate and Nether Gold Ore both work as well. Mine the transmuted ore to obtain Raw Arcane Gold, which can be smelted into Arcane Gold Ingots.

Arcane Gold conducts and stores Wissen, so it appears in nearly every mechanism you will build from here on.
