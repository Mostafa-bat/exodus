<Column alignItems="center" fullWidth={true}>
  ## <Color id="gold">Brilliant Fiber</Color>
</Column>

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="gold_fiber" />
  ### <Color id="aqua">Brilliant Fiber</Color>
</Row>

Brilliant Fiber is the first item you need to craft. Use it on tree leaves and wait for the fiber's energies to spread throughout the canopy. Once the leaves turn fully golden, harvest them for Gold Leaf.


<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

***

<Column alignItems="center" fullWidth={true}>
  ## <Color id="gold">Gold Leaf</Color>
</Column>

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="gold_leaf" />
  ### <Color id="aqua">Gold Leaf</Color>
</Row>

Gold Leaf is the fundamental crafting ingredient for Nature's Aura. Harvest it from leaves that have been fully converted by Brilliant Fiber. You will need plenty of it throughout your progression.

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

***

<Column alignItems="center" fullWidth={true}>
  ## <Color id="gold">Gold Powder</Color>
</Column>

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="gold_powder" />
  ### <Color id="aqua">Gold Powder</Color>
</Row>

Gold Powder is crafted from Gold Leaf and is essential for the Ritual of the Forest. You will need a ring of 16 Gold Powder placed around a sapling each time you perform the ritual.

<Column alignItems="center" fullWidth={true}>
  ## <Color id="gold">Bottled Aura</Color>
</Column>

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="bottle_two_the_rebottling" />
  ### <Color id="aqua">Bottled Aura</Color>
</Row>

Aura saturates the air in different realms. By crafting a Bottle and Cork and using it in various dimensions, you can collect different types of bottled aura.

<Color id="yellow">In this pack the Bottle and Cork takes <ItemLink id="gold_leaf" /> in place of planks</Color>, so Brilliant Fiber comes first. See [Changes in This Pack](pack_changes.md) for the full realm-to-aura map.


There are several types of bottled aura. The bottle you produce depends on the <Color id="green">aura type</Color> of the realm you bottle in:

- <Color id="yellow">Bottled Sunlight</Color> -- collected in overworld-type realms: <Color id="green">Niflheim, Midgard, Asgard, and within Hyperboxes</Color>
- <Color id="gray">Bottled Ghosts</Color> -- collected in nether-type realms: <Color id="green">Muspelheim, Nidhogg's domain, and the Blood Magic Demon Realm</Color>
- <Color id="dark_purple">Bottled Darkness</Color> -- collected in end-type realms: <Color id="green">Ginnungagap, Jotunheim, Alfheim, and on the Bifrost</Color>
- <Color id="dark_gray">Bottled Vacuum</Color> -- obtained in regions where local aura is severely low, regardless of dimension
- <Color id="aqua">Bottled Substance</Color> -- what you get anywhere with no mapped aura type, <Color id="green">Helheim included</Color>. No recipe uses it, so treat it as a sign you are somewhere Nature's Aura does not properly work yet

  ## <Color id="gold">Environmental Eye</Color>
</Column>

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="eye" />
  ### <Color id="aqua">Environmental Eye</Color>
</Row>

A critical tool for working with aura. The Environmental Eye allows you to see the general levels of aura at your location, as well as how much aura is contained in blocks that can hold it. Craft one early and keep it handy.
