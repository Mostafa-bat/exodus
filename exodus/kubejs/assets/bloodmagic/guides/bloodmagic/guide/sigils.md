# <Color id="red">Sigils</Color>

<Column alignItems="center" fullWidth={true}>
  # <Color id="red">Sigils</Color>
  <ItemImage id="bloodmagic:divinationsigil" scale="2" />
  Sigils use LP from your Soul Network to perform a variety of functions and tasks. They are crafted in Alchemy Arrays drawn with Arcane Ashes, using a reagent as the base and a slate as the catalyst.
</Column>

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

Most sigils are toggled with <Color id="yellow">sneak + right-click</Color> and then drain LP on a timer. The ones that act on a single target are just right-click. Curios is installed, so any sigil that has a passive effect can be worn in a charm slot instead of filling your hotbar.

=======

***

<Column alignItems="center" fullWidth={true}>
  ## <Color id="gold">Utility Sigils</Color>
</Column>

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="bloodmagic:divinationsigil" />
  <Column>
    ### <Color id="aqua">Divination Sigil</Color>
  </Column>
</Row>
Right-click a Blood Altar to see its tier, current LP and maximum capacity. Right-click the air to see your Soul Network total. Sneak + right-click opens the HUD editor, where you can drag every Blood Magic overlay to wherever you want it.

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="bloodmagic:seersigil" />
  <Column>
    ### <Color id="aqua">Seer's Sigil</Color>
  </Column>
</Row>
The upgraded Divination Sigil. On top of everything the basic one shows, it adds current crafting progress, LP consumed per tick while crafting, and how much is banked in your Charging Runes.

***

<Column alignItems="center" fullWidth={true}>
  ## <Color id="gold">Elemental Sigils</Color>
</Column>

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="bloodmagic:watersigil" />
  <Column>
    ### <Color id="aqua">Water Sigil</Color>
  </Column>
</Row>

Places a water source block for <Color id="red">100 LP</Color>. If your network is empty it takes the cost out of your health instead. It also stands in for a Water Bucket in any Alchemy Table recipe without being consumed.

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="bloodmagic:lavasigil" />
  <Column>
    ### <Color id="aqua">Lava Sigil</Color>
  </Column>
</Row>

The same idea for lava, at <Color id="red">1,000 LP</Color> a source block. Running dry costs you <Color id="red">five hearts</Color>, so watch your reserves. It can also be used to automate Lava Buckets in the Alchemy Table.

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="bloodmagic:airsigil" />
  <Column>
    ### <Color id="aqua">Air Sigil</Color>
  </Column>
</Row>

Throws you in the direction you are facing for <Color id="red">50 LP</Color> a use.

><Color id="dark_red">Warning:</Color> It gives you no fall protection whatsoever. Running out of LP mid-flight is a well documented way to die.

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="bloodmagic:icesigil" />
  <Column>
    ### <Color id="aqua">Sigil of the Frozen Lake</Color>
  </Column>
</Row>

A toggleable Frost Walker. While it is on, the ground and water under your feet freeze as you move. Toggle it off before you walk over your farm.

***

<Column alignItems="center" fullWidth={true}>
  ## <Color id="gold">Other Sigils</Color>
</Column>

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="bloodmagic:growthsigil" />
  <Column>
    ### <Color id="aqua">Sigil of the Green Grove</Color>
  </Column>
</Row>

Right-click a growable block to apply a bonemeal effect for <Color id="red">150 LP</Color>. Sneak + right-click the air instead and it lights up, applying a growth tick to everything in a 7x7x5 volume around you for <Color id="red">150 LP</Color> every 5 seconds until you switch it off.

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="bloodmagic:miningsigil" />
  <Column>
    ### <Color id="aqua">Sigil of the Fast Miner</Color>
  </Column>
</Row>

Sneak + right-click to toggle Haste on, at <Color id="red">100 LP</Color> every 5 seconds.

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="bloodmagic:sigilofmagnetism" />
  <Column>
    ### <Color id="aqua">Sigil of Magnetism</Color>
  </Column>
</Row>

Sneak + right-click to extend your item pickup range to <Color id="green">7 blocks</Color>, at <Color id="red">50 LP</Color> every 5 seconds.

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="bloodmagic:sigilofholding" />
  <Column>
    ### <Color id="aqua">Sigil of Holding</Color>
  </Column>
</Row>

Holds <Color id="green">5</Color> other sigils and provides their passive effects while they are inside. There are keybinds to open it and to cycle forwards and backwards, and sneak + mouse wheel cycles too.

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="bloodmagic:sigilofsuppression" />
  <Column>
    ### <Color id="aqua">Sigil of Suppression</Color>
  </Column>
</Row>

Right-click to blank out every fluid within roughly 6 blocks of you. The fluid comes straight back once you move away, so do not switch it off while you are at the bottom of the ocean.

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="bloodmagic:bloodlightsigil" />
  <Column>
    ### <Color id="aqua">Sigil of the Blood Lamp</Color>
  </Column>
</Row>

Launches a Blood Light in the direction you are facing. Where it lands, it leaves a nearly invisible light source for <Color id="red">10 LP</Color>.

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="bloodmagic:telepositionsigil" />
  <Column>
    ### <Color id="aqua">Teleposition Sigil</Color>
  </Column>
</Row>

Teleports you to a linked <Color id="green">Teleposer</Color> for <Color id="red">1,000 LP</Color>. Sneak-click a Teleposer with the sigil in hand to record its position and dimension. It binds to the block's location, so moving the Teleposer afterwards breaks the link.

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="bloodmagic:voidsigil" />
  <Column>
    ### <Color id="aqua">Void Sigil</Color>
  </Column>
</Row>

Right-click any fluid to delete it, <Color id="red">50 LP</Color> a block.
