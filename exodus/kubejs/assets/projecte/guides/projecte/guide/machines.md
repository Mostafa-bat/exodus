<Column alignItems="center" fullWidth={true}>
  ## <Color id="gold">Energy Condenser</Color>
</Column>

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<ItemGrid>
  <ItemIcon id="condenser_mk1" />
  <ItemIcon id="condenser_mk2" />
</ItemGrid>

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

The Condenser automatically converts items into a target item. <Color id="gold">Condenser MK2 unlocks once you reach Muspelheim.</Color> Place the target item in the top-left slot (it locks in place). Insert any items with EMC value and they will be destroyed, their EMC used to create copies of the target. The MK2 Condenser is faster and has more slots. Great for automation.
