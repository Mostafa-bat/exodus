***

<Column alignItems="center" fullWidth={true}>
  ## <Color id="gold">EMC Basics</Color>
</Column>

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

Items have an EMC (Energy-Matter Covalence) value. They can be broken down into EMC and rebuilt into other items of equal value. This is the core of equivalent exchange, matter is never created or destroyed, only transformed.

<Color id="yellow">Not everything has EMC here.</Color> Several hundred items have been given a value of zero in this pack, which takes them out of the system completely. Other mods' progression materials in particular are excluded, so EMC will not shortcut them. Check an item in JEI before you build a farm around it, and see [Changes in This Pack](pack_changes.md) for the full picture.

<Column alignItems="center" fullWidth={true}>
  ## <Color id="gold">Philosopher's Stone</Color>
</Column>

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="philosophers_stone" />
  ### <Color id="aqua">Philosopher's Stone</Color>
</Row>

The starting item for ProjectE.

<Color id="yellow">In this pack it is not a crafting table recipe.</Color> The Philosopher's Stone is made with <Color id="dark_purple">Malum Spirit Infusion</Color> from an Ender Pearl, using Eldritch and Arcane spirits plus Hex Ash, Redstone, Soul Stained Steel Nuggets and Rotten Flesh. Malum is effectively the entry requirement for the whole mod.

Press the Extra Function key (default <KeyBind id="key.projecte.fire_projectile" />) while holding it to open a 3x3 crafting grid. It also performs World Transmutation: right-click blocks to cycle them through transmutation chains, and shift-click for alternate outputs.

<Color id="gold">Transmutation has been retuned here.</Color> Sand and sandstone are out of the lists entirely, while sand and gravel now convert into each other, as do Rich Soil and Mycelium. The stone's <Color id="aqua">conversion recipes</Color> (coal to charcoal, diamond to emerald and so on) are removed. See [Changes in This Pack](pack_changes.md).

<Column alignItems="center" fullWidth={true}>
  ## <Color id="gold">Transmutation</Color>
</Column>

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="transmutation_table" />
  ### <Color id="aqua">Transmutation Table</Color>
</Row>

Place items in the Transmutation Table to "learn" them - you can then convert learned items into each other using their EMC values. Items are destroyed and their EMC is added to your personal EMC pool. You can then withdraw items from the pool as long as you have enough EMC.

<Color id="gold">The Table unlocks once you reach Muspelheim</Color>, so early ProjectE runs on the Philosopher's Stone, Collectors, Relays and the Condenser instead.


<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="transmutation_tablet" />
  ### <Color id="aqua">Transmutation Tablet</Color>
</Row>

Portable version of the Transmutation Table. Shares the same knowledge and EMC pool - use it anywhere without needing to return to your base. Unlocks once you reach <Color id="red">Midgard</Color>.

</Column>

The two advanced materials that form the backbone of high-tier ProjectE crafting. Both unlock once you reach <Color id="gold">Muspelheim</Color>.

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="dark_matter" />
  ### <Color id="aqua">Dark Matter</Color>
</Row>

<Color id="dark_purple">Dark Matter</Color> is crafted from Aeternalis Fuel and diamond.


<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="red_matter" />
  ### <Color id="aqua">Red Matter</Color>
</Row>

<Color id="dark_purple">Red Matter</Color> is crafted from Dark Matter and Aeternalis Fuel, and is the base of the Project Expansion matter chain.

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<Color id="yellow">These are crafting materials only in this pack.</Color> The Dark Matter and Red Matter tools, armour and furnaces are all removed, so the matters exist to build machines and to feed the Project Expansion tiers. See [Changes in This Pack](pack_changes.md).
