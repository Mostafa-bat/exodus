  # <Color id="dark_purple">ProjectE</Color>

  <ItemImage id="red_matter" scale="2" />

  ProjectE is an alchemy and equivalent exchange mod. Every item has an EMC (Energy-Matter Covalence) value. Learn items in a Transmutation Table, then convert between items of equal value. Generate EMC passively with Energy Collectors, store it in Klein Stars, condense items automatically with Energy Condensers, and craft a large range of magical rings and amulets.
</Column>

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<Color id="red">ProjectE is heavily modified in this pack.</Color> The EMC table is rebalanced, several hundred items have no EMC at all, most early recipes are rebuilt, and the Dark Matter and Red Matter tool and armour lines are removed. Read [Changes in This Pack](pack_changes.md) first.
